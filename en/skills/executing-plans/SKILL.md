# Executing plans

Turn an agreed plan into a delivery that can be checked line by line.

## Method

- Read the complete plan critically before starting. Verify what you can yourself; apply the gate to doubts that need the user’s ruling, and proceed with authorized steps that do not depend on them.
- Follow the goal, scope, and trade-offs the plan fixes; implementation details within that scope are yours to decide.
- Complete the items in dependency order; independent steps may run in parallel. Report the result of each item on delivery.

## Gate

When a case is not covered by the plan or a step cannot proceed, first check it against the agreed scope, deliverable, and authorization. If it would exceed the scope, change the deliverable, take an irreversible or high-consequence action, or need input only the user has, pause only the actions that depend on that decision, state what you tried and what is missing, and continue the remaining authorized work. Decide low-cost reversible implementation details within scope yourself, and record the decision and its basis wherever you actually deviate from the plan. For a trade-off whose owner is unclear, follow §3 of the standing principles.

## Completion criterion

Check every plan item against fresh evidence and report every deviation between the plan and the result, including those you decided yourself; include the supporting evidence for each item reported as complete.

# Executing plans

Turn an agreed plan into a delivery that can be checked line by line.

## Method

- Read the complete plan critically before acting. Surface and resolve doubts before starting.
- Do only what the plan specifies. If a situation is not covered, stop and ask rather than filling in a decision yourself.

## Gate

When blocked by a missing dependency, unclear instruction, or repeated verification failure, stop and return the question to the user; do not work around it by guessing.

## Completion criterion

Check every plan item against fresh evidence and report every deviation between the plan and the result.

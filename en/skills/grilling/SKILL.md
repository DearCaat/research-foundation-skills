# Grilling

Converge discussion into an unambiguous, user-approved decision record.

## Method

- Ask one question at a time; wait for the answer before asking the next. Do not bundle decisions.
- Verify facts from documents, data, and literature yourself. Escalate only preferences, private information, authority, or other user-exclusive trade-offs.
- Before asking, check whether existing context and decisions already settle the point. If they do, decide it yourself and record the reason.
- For genuine choice points, present 2–3 concrete options, identify your recommendation and its reason, and let the user choose.

## Gate

Do not write, modify, or run anything until every user-owned decision is settled. If the topic has not been brainstormed, use `/brainstorming` first.

## Completion criterion

Every user-exclusive decision has a user-confirmed answer; every agent-owned decision has a recorded decision and evidence or rationale.

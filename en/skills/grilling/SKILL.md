# Grilling

Converge discussion into an unambiguous, user-approved decision record.

## Method

- Ask one question at a time; wait for the answer before asking the next. Do not bundle decisions.
- Ask only questions whose answer would change what gets done and that only the user can answer: name what each question needs from the user—a preference, private information, a decision within their authority, or acceptance of an irreversible consequence. Decide and record yourself whatever can be looked up, derived, or is already settled; the boundary follows §3 of the standing principles.
- For genuine choice points, present 2–3 concrete options, identify your recommendation and its reason, and let the user choose.

## Gate

An open trade-off blocks only the actions that depend on it; verification, trial calculations, and already-authorized work that do not depend on it continue. Use `/brainstorming` first when design-changing alternatives have not yet been compared. Once the options are ready for a decision, or only user-exclusive input is missing, ask directly.

## Completion criterion

Every user-exclusive decision has a user-confirmed answer; every agent-owned decision has a recorded decision and evidence or rationale.
